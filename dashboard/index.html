DupeLens

The same product is often listed many times by different sellers at different prices. DupeLens reads each listing's photo and title, finds the other listings of the same product, and shows how its price compares with the typical (median) price.

Price lookup: Amazon India, personal care appliances

Per-listing F1 (Kaggle convention, self-match counted) with 95% CIs from bootstrapping product groups. Trained and tuned on separate product groups; no product appears in two splits.
